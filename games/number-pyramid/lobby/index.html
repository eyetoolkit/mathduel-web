How it works

1Each brick equals the sum of the two bricks directly beneath it
2Given bricks (paper) are fixed — only the blanks are yours to fill
3Pencil marks let you note candidates; toggle Notes (✏) to jot them
4Duel: you and the bot each own half the bricks — first side done wins
