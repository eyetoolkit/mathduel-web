How to playLook → place → smile.

1Find a row or boxPick any row, column or 2×2 box that's almost full — it's the easiest place to start.
2Spot the missing digitWhich of 1–4 is missing? If only one digit can fit, that's your answer!
3Tap and placeTap the cell, tap the number. Duplicates light up instantly so mistakes never stick.
4Finish and celebrateFill every cell to complete the grid — then try a harder level or a timed sprint.
