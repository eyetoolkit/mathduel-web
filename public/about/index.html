ABOUT

Six math games. Open them and play.

MathDuel is a static little site with a simple goal: give you a genuinely good math puzzle in under two seconds — no download, no sign-up, no paywall, no ad breaks.

What you can play today

24 GameMake 24 from four cards. Daily, timed, or head-to-head. Sudoku 9×9Classic grid, three difficulty levels, pencil marks included. Sudoku 6×6The same reasoning, beginner-friendly board. Killer SudokuCaged sums on top of standard Sudoku rules. Equation PyramidFill the base so every brick computes upward. Number Guess 1A2BGuess the hidden number from A/B feedback.

Every puzzle is generated deterministically from a date seed, so everyone in the world gets the same daily board — that is what makes the daily race fair.

Three rules we stick to

  • No account required. Anything playable is reachable without logging in. Progress like preferences is kept locally in your own browser.
  • No ads, no trackers. We do not run advertising scripts or sell behavioural data.
  • No dark patterns. No coin pop-ups mid-puzzle, no countdown timers on paywalls.

The Duel family

MathDuel is one of three sibling sites: BoardDuel for board games and MemoryDuel for knowledge battles. Same design language, same no-nonsense philosophy.

Found something broken or want a board you miss? Tell us — bug reports genuinely shape what gets fixed next.
