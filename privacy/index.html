Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026 · Version 1.0

MathDuel is part of the unified tri-sites platform (numeriduel.com, boardduel.com, memoryduel.com). This privacy policy covers all three sites. We follow a strict privacy-first principle: collect as little as possible, never collect personally identifiable information from students, and delete data automatically.

1. What We Collect

MathDuel collects only the following anonymous data:

  • Anonymous identifier (UUID) — auto-generated by your browser when you create or join a classroom. Not tied to your real name, email, or school.
  • Game outcome data — match type, win/loss/draw, completion time, ELO change.
  • Teacher preferences — opt-in only, stored in your browser's localStorage, never on our servers.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Real names, student IDs, email addresses, phone numbers, parent contacts
  • ❌ Government-issued IDs (passport, national ID, etc.)
  • ❌ Biometric data (face, fingerprint, voiceprint, retina) — biometric identifiers are not collected by any COPPA function
  • ❌ IP addresses, device fingerprints, Advertising IDs
  • ❌ Precise geolocation
  • ❌ Address book, camera, microphone input

3. Why We Collect (Purpose Limitation)

The anonymous UUID and game data are used solely for these "support for internal operations" purposes (16 CFR § 312.5(c)(2)):

  1. Cross-lesson ELO tracking — so teachers see the same cohort's progress over weeks
  2. Anti-cheat — detect repeat-join from the same anonymous user in the same room
  3. Engine improvement — aggregated anonymous data for performance bottlenecks
  4. Session replay (BoardDuel only) — read from in-memory state, never persisted to KV

We never use UUIDs for behavioral profiling, advertising, re-marketing, cross-site identification, or marketing email/SMS to students or parents.

4. Third Parties Who Receive Data

We share data with only the following categories of third parties (COPPA § 312.4 amended requirement):

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — Workers Paid Plan hosts our edge code, KV (90-day TTL), D1 databases, R2 buckets, and Durable Objects. Cloudflare acts as our service provider; they store data on our behalf only. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub, Inc. — stores our source code repository (no PII).
  • Agent Mail (智邮) — provides our contact email alias; we do not store email content on our servers.

We do not sell, rent, share, or exchange data with any advertising networks, data brokers, social media platforms, analytics services beyond Cloudflare Workers Logs, or any other third parties.

5. Data Retention

All collected data is automatically deleted after 90 days via Cloudflare KV TTL. There is no manual override and no "indefinite retention". Teacher localStorage is cleared when the teacher clears their browser data.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

MathDuel may be used by teachers with students under 13. We follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506; 16 CFR § 312) including the Final Amendments published 2025-01-16 with compliance deadline 2026-04-22. Because we do not collect personal information from children — only anonymous UUIDs and game outcome data — most COPPA requirements do not apply. The exceptions (written data retention policy, written information security program, persistent-identifier disclosure) are satisfied by this document and the linked compliance policies.

We do not display advertising to children. We do not send marketing communications to children. We do not condition participation on collecting more information than necessary.

8. Your Rights (Parents & Teachers)

You may at any time:

  • Access — email mathduel@agent.qq.com with the room code or approximate date; we reply with the anonymous UUIDs involved.
  • Delete — same email; we purge the related KV keys within 48 hours.
  • Opt out — teachers can disable cloud ELO sync in the teacher console; data stays in localStorage only.
  • Contact the operator — mathduel@agent.qq.com (this is also the DSAR contact required by 16 CFR § 312.6).

9. Cross-Site Data Isolation

Although numeriduel.com, boardduel.com, and memoryduel.com are operated by the same team, the UUID namespaces are fully isolated across the three sites. We do not link a student's anonymous identity across sites. A teacher cannot see a student's data on one site based on their activity on another.

10. International Users

Although we are privacy-first by design, we acknowledge that anonymous UUIDs may be considered "pseudonymous" under the EU GDPR rather than "anonymous". We do not process data for behavioral profiling, do not transfer data outside of Cloudflare's global edge, and do not respond to automated data subject access requests from third parties. EU/UK users may still exercise the rights in § 8.

11. Changes to This Policy

Material changes are announced in the site footer for at least 30 days before they take effect. The current version is v1.0 · 2026-09-30. Next review: 2027-09-30.

12. Regulatory Complaint

If you believe we have not complied with COPPA, you may file a complaint with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission:

数据收集范围

我们仅收集：① 浏览器自动生成的匿名 UUID（玩家在创建/加入房间时）；② 游戏结果（胜负/用时/ELO 变化）；③ 教师可选的浏览器偏好（localStorage，不写入服务端）。

我们不收集：真实姓名、学号、邮箱、手机号、家长联系方式；政府签发身份证件；任何生物识别数据（面部、指纹、声纹、虹膜等）；IP 地址、设备指纹、Advertising ID；精确地理位置；通讯录、相机、麦克风输入。

数据使用目的

仅用于：① 跨课时 ELO 追踪 ② 防作弊 ③ 引擎性能改进 ④ 复盘回放（BoardDuel 专属，从 DO 内存读取）。绝不用于行为画像、广告、再营销、跨站识别、或向学生/家长发送营销信息。

第三方共享

我们仅向以下类别第三方共享数据：① Cloudflare（Workers Paid Plan 边缘托管、KV、D1、R2、Durable Objects）；② GitHub（源代码仓库，不含 PII）；③ Agent Mail 智邮（联系邮箱别名）。我们不向任何广告网络、数据中介、社交平台或分析服务出售/出租/共享数据。

所有数据通过 Cloudflare KV TTL 90 天后自动删除。无人工覆盖、无"无限期留存"。教师 localStorage 在浏览器数据清除时一并清除。

我们遵守美国《儿童在线隐私保护法》（COPPA）2025 年 1 月 16 日最终修正案（含 2026-04-22 合规截止日）。由于我们不收集儿童 PII（仅匿名 UUID 与游戏结果），COPPA 大部分要求不适用。例外要求（书面留存政策、书面信息安全计划、持久标识符披露）已通过本文档及指向 compliance/ 的链接满足。
